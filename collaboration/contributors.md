## Coordination to establish

- One point of contact and reviewer for each active workstream.
- Locations of existing code, calculation files, and reports.
- Agreed component-interface versions and a process for proposing changes.
- Student handover expectations, including runnable calculations and explanatory notes.
- Publication and reuse terms for contributed material before a public release.

Use the central decision record for decisions affecting more than one component. Keep implementation discussions with the repository that owns the work, linking to the central record when necessary.
