## Existing analysis environment

The November 2025 UM report describes OpenMC and deterministic analysis tools including REBUS, DIF3D, MC2-3, and PERSENT, together with STAR-CCM+ and ANSYS Fluent studies. It also documents coupled point-kinetics and thermal-fluid calculations for online refueling. The project owner reports that the team also uses SAM. Do not infer the tool used for each new accident case until its study record is available.

The report is UM/NRDSL-25/02, *Core Design Evolution of Wielenga Innovation Salt Tank Reactor (WISTR)*, by Won Sik Yang, Hangyu Lee, Ryan Willat, and Thomas Wielenga, dated November 15, 2025. See W10 for its original file and older revision.

## Fields for a new study record

Record its question, responsible authors, design revision, inputs and assumptions, software versions, case identifiers, source location, report citation, findings and limitations, review status, and downstream uses. An ongoing study should not be listed as a completed result.

For CFD-derived correlations or other reduced descriptions, record the conditions under which they were obtained and where they are used. A change to the modeled geometry should trigger an applicability review.
