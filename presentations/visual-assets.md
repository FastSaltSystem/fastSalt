The installation now contains the preserved detailed reactor at full scale, seated with its caps at operating-deck level. Six coolant routes match its actual nozzles and connect to the three illustrative steam-generator envelopes. The deck opening and foundation extension have been adjusted for the larger well. The hot and cold hood ducts remain short stubs for later connection.

The reactor images were refreshed from the preserved 2026-10-08 handling-and-plug scene, including the current hooks and circular plugs. Existing earlier images in `outputs/reactor-study/` were retained.

## Remaining presentation work

- Develop the external steam-power-plant context (turbine/condenser and their connections) as a clearly provisional arrangement; it is not present in these reactor-building views.
- Select one sourced UM research figure and incorporate any available recent UM/MSU updates.
- Compose the slide deck using these views with fewer, larger labels where needed. The technical render annotations are reference information, not a requirement to reproduce every label on a slide.
- Preserve the final assets with the delivered deck. The current working folders are excluded from Git and are reproducible using the [installation guide](../../fastSaltModels/docs/installation.md) and [reactor guide](../../fastSaltModels/docs/reactor-study.md).

Geometric checks are recorded in the [installation report](../../fastSaltModels/outputs/workshop-installation/wistr-model-report.json). They cover placement, connections, deck openings, and driver validity. They do not qualify pipe bends, supports, the full maintenance route, or plant performance.
